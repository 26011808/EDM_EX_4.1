Ex No. 4.1: CUT SECTIONS OF SOLIDS

AIM:

To create the solid model from the given drawing and section the solid model.

REQUIREMENTS:
 * Autodesk Fusion 360
 * Windows 10, 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommended 1 GB or more)
 * 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload
   
PROCEDURE:
Step 1: Study the Given Drawing
 * Carefully examine the isometric view to understand the overall shape of the component.
 * Identify the different features:
   * A flat base plate with rounded ends.
   * A raised cylindrical boss at the centre.
   * A central through-hole.
   * A counterbore at the top of the central hole.
   * Two mounting holes on the base.
 * Study the Top View, Front View, and Sectional View to understand the dimensions and positions of all features.
 * Note all the dimensions given in the drawing.
 * Select a suitable drawing scale according to the drawing sheet.

Step 2: Prepare the Drawing Sheet
 * Fix the drawing sheet properly on the drawing board.
 * Draw the border and title block according to the standard engineering drawing format.
 * Select suitable positions for the orthographic views and isometric view.
 * Draw light horizontal and vertical reference lines using a T-square or mini drafter.
 * Maintain sufficient spacing between the views for proper dimensioning.

Step 3: Draw the Top View
 * Draw the horizontal and vertical centre lines of the component.
 * Draw the outer profile of the base plate, including its rounded ends and curved edges.
 * Mark the centre point of the raised cylindrical boss.
 * Draw three concentric circles representing the boss and central hole:
   * Outer boss diameter: 50 mm
   * Central bore diameter: 20 mm
   * Counterbore diameter: 30 mm
 * Locate the two mounting holes on the base using the given dimensions.
 * Draw both mounting holes with a diameter of 12 mm. (Note: The drawing sheet on page 8 shows Ø15 for the side holes, 100 overall length, 47.92 width, 23.96 offset, R15, and heights 38 and 29.)
 * Draw centre lines through all circular features.
 * Check the alignment and symmetry of the entire Top View.

Step 4: Draw the Front View
 * Project vertical lines from the Top View according to the selected projection method.
 * Draw the base plate with a thickness of 10 mm.
 * Draw the raised cylindrical boss above the base plate.
 * Mark the height of the boss as 28 mm above the base plate.
 * Draw the upper counterbore and central bore according to the sectional dimensions.
 * Represent the mounting holes using hidden lines wherever necessary.
 * Draw centre lines through the boss and holes.
 * Darken the visible outlines after checking all dimensions.

Step 5: Draw the Sectional View
 * Identify the cutting plane shown in the given engineering drawing.
 * Draw the sectional view by projecting the required features from the other views.
 * Represent the central bore and counterbore accurately.
 * Draw the counterbore with a diameter of 30 mm and a depth of 6 mm.
 * Draw the central through-hole with a diameter of 20 mm.
 * Represent the mounting holes and their positions in the base plate.
 * Apply uniform section hatching to the solid material cut by the section plane.
 * Do not hatch the holes, bores, or empty spaces.
 * Use thin lines for hatching and thicker lines for visible outlines.
   
Step 6: Draw the Isometric View
 * Draw the isometric axes: one vertical axis and two axes inclined at 30° to the horizontal.
 * Construct the base plate in isometric form using the dimensions from the orthographic views.
 * Draw the rounded ends and curved edges of the base plate.
 * Construct the raised cylindrical boss on the base plate.
 * Represent the circular top surfaces as isometric ellipses.
 * Draw the central bore and counterbore as concentric isometric ellipses.
 * Show the two mounting holes on the base plate.
 * Draw all visible edges using continuous thick lines.
 * Remove unwanted construction lines and complete the isometric representation.
   
Step 7: Dimensioning and Finishing
 * Add the overall dimensions and individual feature dimensions.
 * Indicate the diameters of all circular features using the Ø symbol.
 * Mark the radius of the rounded ends using the R symbol.
 * Add centre lines and hidden lines wherever necessary.
 * Ensure that all dimensions are clear and do not overlap.
 * Check that the Top, Front, Sectional, and Isometric Views match one another.
 * Darken the final outlines and erase unnecessary construction lines.
 * Complete the title block with the drawing title, scale, date, and other required details.
